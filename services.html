How does ZenixZ AI work 24/7?
ZenixZ AI operates continuously on cloud infrastructure via Botpress, ensuring your midnight customers get instant answers without human downtime.
Can it integrate with Telegram & WhatsApp?
Yes! Under our Pro Business plan ($149), customer orders and inquiries are automatically routed straight to your personal Telegram or WhatsApp messenger instantly.
What are the deployment pricing plans?
We offer transparent pricing: Starter Bot is $49 setup fee for simple FAQs, and Pro Business Bot is $149 setup fee featuring full messaging integration.
How long does bot setup take?
Typically, standard Starter bots are deployed within 24-48 hours, while custom Botpress Pro Business setups take 3-5 business days including training.
Can the bot speak Sinhala and Tamil?
Absolutely! Our Botpress models support multi-language NLP intent training, allowing local customers to chat comfortably in Sinhala, Tamil, or English.
What if the AI doesn't know an answer?
We configure a seamless Live Chat Handoff feature. If the bot encounters an unknown query, it instantly alerts your human team via email or Telegram.
Do I need coding knowledge to manage it?
Not at all! We provide an intuitive dashboard where you can review conversation logs, update product details, and monitor customer engagement effortlessly.
